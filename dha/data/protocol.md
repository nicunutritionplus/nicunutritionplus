# Protocol: Enteral DHA and/or ARA supplementation in preterm infants — systematic review of RCTs

Date of protocol: 2026-11-10 (search date identical)

## Review question
In infants born preterm, does enteral supplementation with docosahexaenoic acid (DHA) and/or arachidonic acid (ARA) — compared with placebo, standard (unsupplemented) formula or no supplementation — change neonatal morbidity, mortality, growth, visual function or neurodevelopment?

## Eligibility (PICOS)
- **Population**: infants born < 37 weeks' gestational age (including VLBW < 1500 g and ELBW < 1000 g), in hospital or after discharge.
- **Intervention**: enteral DHA and/or ARA, by any vehicle: (a) LCPUFA-supplemented preterm or post-discharge formula; (b) direct enteral emulsion/oil (e.g., DHA/ARA oils, fish-oil emulsions); (c) high-dose DHA given to lactating mothers to enrich breast milk fed to the preterm infant.
- **Comparator**: placebo/control oil, unsupplemented formula, standard care, or a lower-dose DHA regimen (dose-comparison trials are included and flagged).
- **Design**: randomised controlled trials (individual or cluster). Quasi-randomised, non-randomised and observational studies excluded.
- **Reporting status**: (i) trials with results published in a peer-reviewed journal; (ii) registered trials that are ongoing (recruiting, not yet recruiting, active not recruiting, enrolling by invitation) or completed without posted results, listed as ongoing/awaiting publication. Conference abstracts only are excluded from synthesis.
- **Exclusions**: parenteral lipid emulsion trials (fish-oil/SMOF vs soybean) where LCPUFA is not given enterally; term infants; pregnancy/antenatal supplementation only; secondary follow-up reports are linked to their parent trial rather than counted as separate trials.

## Outcomes
Primary: bronchopulmonary dysplasia (BPD, oxygen at 36 weeks PMA); retinopathy of prematurity (any and severe, ≥ stage 3 or treated); neurodevelopment (Bayley MDI/cognitive score at 18–24 months).
Secondary: necrotising enterocolitis (NEC ≥ stage II), late-onset sepsis, mortality before discharge, growth (weight, length, head circumference), visual acuity, later IQ/cognition, adverse events.

## Methods
Search: PubMed (E-utilities), Europe PMC, CrossRef/OpenAlex, ClinicalTrials.gov (API v2); citation chasing from included trials and prior systematic reviews.
Screening: title/abstract then full text, one reviewer (AI-assisted), exclusion reasons recorded.
Risk of bias: Cochrane RoB 2 domains, assessed from published reports.
Synthesis: random-effects meta-analysis (REML, Hartung–Knapp adjustment) of risk ratios for dichotomous outcomes reported by ≥ 3 trials; subgroup by DHA alone vs DHA+ARA and by vehicle; narrative synthesis otherwise. Certainty assessed with GRADE-style criteria.
